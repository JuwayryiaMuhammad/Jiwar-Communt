# 0094 — Purchase orders, goods and service receipts and the three-way match: an order raised directly, from an approved request or from a tender's award, approved by another and kept as the one commitment of its budget line, amended by revisions, sent as a PDF and acknowledged by the vendor; receipts in parts into the store; a vendor's invoice matched to the order and what was accepted, within the compound's tolerances

**Status:** Accepted · Phase M3-7 · amends ADR 0010 (four permissions), 0055 (the thresholds also bind an order), 0065 (a receipt's reference gets a link), 0077 ("No purchase order" is over), 0086 (three reports), 0088 (an award can lead to an order; the contractor sees its orders), 0089 (a third source of an invoice, a third source of a commitment), 0092 (what the ledger does not post), 0093 (an order's lines carry VAT and withholding categories)

## Context

ADR 0077 built purchase requests as a light first step ("no purchase order"); ADR 0089 left "purchase orders, goods receipts and the three-way match" open; ADR 0065 left a stock receipt's reference as free text. The owner decided on 2026-11-10 to build them as the finance phase after taxes. The owner gave the shape (raised from a request, an award or directly; approval by ADR 0055's thresholds; the same commitment, not a second; revisions; receipts in parts; tolerances as a compound setting; a mismatch blocks payment approval until resolved or overridden with a reason; nobody approves their own order or overrides their own match) and no numbers. **Wherever a rate, a limit or a rule is open it is a compound setting with a default recorded below as an assumption.**

## Decisions

### Where it lives

- **Orders and receipts are maintenance's** (`src/maintenance/purchase-orders`), beside the requests, the tenders, the vendors and the stock they touch. **The match and the tolerances are finance's** (`src/finance/payables/invoice-match.*`, `match-rules.ts`), beside the invoices. They meet where ADR 0089's bridge already meets: core's `PayablesBridge` gains `commitments.sync` for a third source (`purchase_order`), and `PayableSources.order` (the order with its lines and what was accepted of each, locked for update) and `orderInvoiced` (after an invoice is approved: the order closes if it is complete). Finance imports no maintenance code; maintenance imports no finance code (maintenance reads the invoices' and settings' tables by query, as it already reads the budget lines).

### Permissions (amends ADR 0010)

- **Reused:** `purchases.approve` approves or turns down an order and a revision (ADR 0077's approvers, a live deputy among them); `finance.payables` records the invoice and reads the match, the settings and the reports; `finance.payables_approve` approves the invoice and keeps the tolerances; `finance.budget` is the budget line's.
- **New:** `purchase_orders.raise` (staff, manager; the manager, the accountant and the maintenance supervisor) raises, amends, sends and cancels the rest of an order; `purchase_orders.receive` (the manager and the maintenance supervisor) records receipts; `finance.match_override` (staff, manager; the manager; a `finance.*`, so a deputy never holds it) lets a mismatch through; `purchase_orders.acknowledge` (the contractor kind; the contractor role) reads its company's orders and acknowledges them. `access:sync` brings them to existing compounds.
- **Nobody approves their own order, and nobody overrides their own match.** Who raised an order — or asked for the revision that waits — never approves or turns it down, **even under the board's approved major contract** (stricter than a purchase request's, the rule ADR 0089 already took for invoices): a compound with a single manager gives `purchase_orders.raise` to the accountant. Who recorded an invoice never overrides its match (`MATCH_SELF_OVERRIDE`); the approver of an invoice is still not its recorder (ADR 0089).

### The order (`purchase_orders`, `purchase_order_lines`, `purchase_order_revisions`; PO- numbers)

- **A vendor** of the register (not suspended), **a budget line**, **a delivery day** (not before today), a note, and 1 to 50 **lines**: a `stock` line names an item (its unit is the line's; received into a store), a `goods` line a free description (received, not stocked), a `service` line a description (confirmed done); each has a quantity (three places), a unit (ADR 0065's stock units and `lot`, `hour`, `day`, `month`), a **net unit price** (two places), a **VAT code** (`zero`, `exempt`, `vat_14`, as a vendor invoice's lines, ADR 0089, 0093) and a **withholding category** (ADR 0093, optional). A line's net is its quantity at its price to the piastre, its VAT the net at the code's rate to the piastre (half up); **net, VAT and gross** are the sums; a CHECK repeats the arithmetic and the lines add up to the order at commit.
- **Raised** (`POST /staff/purchase-orders`, idempotent) **directly**; **from an approved purchase request** (`purchaseRequestId`: its vendor and line are the defaults; one live order a request, by a unique key; `already_ordered`, `request_not_approved`, `vendor_mismatch`); or **from an awarded tender** (`tenderId`: the award's vendor; the orders raised from one award add up to at most the winning bid, `award_exceeded`; refused when the award's contract keeps an unpaid payment schedule, which already reserves its line, `contract_scheduled`; `tender_not_awarded`). Waiting for an approver; the approvers are told (`purchase_order.submitted`) and it is in the staff inbox (type `purchase_order`, 72 h, against the major contract threshold; never in bulk).
- **Approved** (`POST …/approve`) by a `purchases.approve` holder who did not raise it. Up to the manager's limit (`major_contract`'s threshold, ADR 0055, always binding, ADR 0073) on the order's **gross**; above it only under the board's approved major contract naming a cost of at least the gross (`PURCHASE_ORDER_NEEDS_APPROVAL`, `PURCHASE_ORDER_APPROVAL_INVALID` `not_approved | below_amount | covers_another`), which signs **one order, request or contract** (the claim lock of ADR 0077). An approval its request or award, or an earlier revision, was approved under serves the order when it covers the amount. Turned down (`…/reject`, a `purchaseOrderReject` code) or called off while waiting (`…/cancel`, a `purchaseOrderCancel` code, by its raiser or an approver). Forward only (a trigger): requested → approved | rejected | cancelled; approved → closed.
- **One commitment, not a second** (ADR 0089). Approving keeps the line's money for the order's gross, in the same transaction, within what the line has available (`BUDGET_LINE_INSUFFICIENT`). An order raised from a request **takes the request's commitment over**: the request's is released (the new release reason `ordered`), the order's reserved, and the request marked fulfilled with the order's number; a request a live order names cannot be called off or fulfilled by hand. An invoice's approval releases its total from the order's commitment (the reason `invoiced`), as for a request; what an order that closed has left is given back (`closed`); a cancelled remainder (`cancelled`) and a revision (`changed`) as ADR 0089. The cash flow forecast and the payment schedule count it as they count a request's.
- **Amended** (`POST …/amend`, a `purchaseOrderAmend` code, the whole order as amended): a **new revision**, approved again by the same rules (on the new gross); one waits at a time (`PURCHASE_ORDER_REVISION_PENDING`); the revision in force stands until it is approved; its asker may take it back (`…/withdraw-revision`); turned down, the order stands. **No line goes below what was accepted or invoiced of it, and a line with a receipt or an invoice is never dropped** (`PURCHASE_ORDER_LINE_BELOW_USED {min}`); the vendor never changes; a line keeps its position. Approving a revision re-keeps the commitment at the new gross less what approved invoices took. Each revision keeps the proposal whole (a snapshot of its lines), so the history stays readable.
- **Cancelling what is left** (`…/cancel` on an approved order, a `purchaseOrderCancel` code): the order **closes** (`remainder_cancelled`); what was not received is cancelled and its commitment given back; **what arrived and is not invoiced yet stays committed** (its quantity at the order's price with its VAT) until it is invoiced, then the rest is released. A waiting revision is withdrawn.
- **Closed** by itself (`completed`) once every line is accepted and invoiced at its ordered quantity; the tail of its commitment is given back.
- **Sent** (`POST …/send`): records the revision sent and tells the vendor's contractors (`purchase_order.sent`); **the PDF** (`GET …/pdf`, bilingual, drawn on demand from the revision in force, never stored) is what the office sends the vendor and what its contractors download. **Acknowledged** by a contractor of the vendor (`POST /contractor/purchase-orders/{id}/acknowledge`): the revision sent; a new revision asks again. A contractor reads only the orders sent to its company (what was ordered, its prices and delivery day; never the budget, approvals, receipts or another company's, the same 404 as an unknown one). A contractor may also record an invoice against one of its company's orders.

### Receipts (`goods_receipts`, `goods_receipt_lines`, `goods_receipt_photos`; GR- numbers)

- `POST …/receipts` (idempotent) records what arrived against an **approved** order, in parts: per line the quantity **accepted** and the quantity **rejected** (a rejection has a `goodsRejection` code, and only a rejection has one), a note; the receipt's day (not ahead), the vendor's delivery note, a note, up to 10 photos (`goods_receipt_photo` files, images of 10 MB). Append-only.
- **At most the ordered quantity** (plus the compound's quantity tolerance, below) is accepted in all (`GOODS_RECEIPT_EXCEEDS_ORDER {max}`); a rejected quantity is not received, so the line still waits for it.
- **A stock line accepted** is received into a store (`storeId`, required with one and refused without) through ADR 0065's one writer of levels, **at the order's unit price** (so the item's average cost moves as for any receipt), and the movement **links the receipt** (`stock_movements.goods_receipt_id`); the movement's free-text reference is the receipt's number. A **goods** or **service** line is confirmed received. Holders of `purchase_orders.receive` receive anything; the order's raiser, or its request's requester, may confirm **service lines** alone (a service is confirmed by whoever asked for it).
- Fully received and invoiced, the order closes.

### Received but not invoiced: not accrued in the ledger (a decision)

**The ledger does not accrue a receipt.** Reasons: (1) ADR 0092's books are accrual at the invoice's approval, and stock movements and ticket costs are cost records the ledger does not post — there is no inventory account to debit, and a goods-received-not-invoiced liability without one would be half a mechanism; (2) the budget is already held: the order's commitment keeps the money of what arrived and is not yet invoiced, so the line's `available` never overstates; (3) accruing at receipt means revaluing at the invoice (a price variance account, stock at a cost that differs from the average cost), which is a stock-valuation policy the owner has not set. So **"received but not invoiced" is a report**, `po_received_not_invoiced`, read from the registers, and the invoice posts as ADR 0092 and 0093 say (the line's category — less the VAT recovered — against payables). When stock valuation is built, a system account for goods received not invoiced would be added to the chart then.

### The three-way match (`finance_invoice_match_overrides`; settings on `finance_settings`)

- **An invoice names an order** (`purchaseOrderId` beside `contractId` and `purchaseRequestId`; exactly one of the three): its vendor, line and the board's approval are the order's; the order is approved or closed (`order_not_open`); a request an order took over is invoiced through the order (`ordered`). Its **lines name the order's lines** with a quantity and a unit price (the amount is their product, to the piastre; its VAT code defaults to the order line's; its withholding category defaults to that of the order's largest line that names one), or carry an amount and name none (freight). Each order line once.
- **The match is read live**, never stored: the order's lines, what was accepted of each, and what the other invoices of the order billed that count before this one (approved or paid, or received with a lower number — so the first recorded keeps the quantity). `GET /staff/finance/vendor-invoices/{id}/match` shows the invoice's lines beside the order's, the issues, the price variance and the tolerances.
- **Issues** (`quantity_not_received`: billed beyond what was accepted; `quantity_over_order`: beyond what was ordered; `price_over`: a unit price above the order's by more than the price percentage **or** a line varying by more than the amount; `extra_line`: a line naming no order line above the amount). A **lower price is never an issue** (the compound's gain, reported as a negative variance).
- **Within tolerance → the invoice goes on to approval as any. An issue blocks its approval** (`409 INVOICE_MATCH_BLOCKED {issues}`) until it is **resolved** — by **a receipt** (the match is live, nothing to do), by **a corrected invoice or the vendor's credit note** (the invoice is called off with the reason `corrected`, which frees the vendor's number, and the corrected one recorded) — or **let through** (`POST …/match/override`, a `matchOverride` code, `finance.match_override`, once, by someone who did not record the invoice, while it waits; audited with the issues as kinds, positions, quantities and amounts).
- **Price variance within tolerance posts where ADR 0092 puts the cost: in the line's budget category.** The invoice's approval debits the category (less VAT recovered) for the invoice's *total*, whatever the order said, so the variance is simply more expense of that line and is reported (`priceVariance`, the report); there is no price-variance account because the books carry no stock or order accrual (above). The commitment's release is capped at what is open and the approval checks that the line has the rest available.
- **The settings** (`GET|PATCH /staff/finance/purchase-order-settings`; read with either payables permission, kept with `finance.payables_approve`, audited): **price tolerance 2 %**, **quantity tolerance 0 %** (a percentage of the ordered, and of the accepted, quantity — also what a receipt may accept over the order), **amount tolerance 100 EGP**, **an order required above: never** (null).
- **A compound setting decides when an order is required.** Default off: invoices without an order keep working as today. Set to an amount, an invoice **with no contract** (a purchase request's) above it needs an order (`409 VENDOR_INVOICE_PO_REQUIRED {above}`); contract invoices keep their own approval and are exempt.

### Reports (ADR 0086), for `finance.payables`, on screen, PDF, workbook and CSV, compared and scheduled

`po_open` (approved, not closed, raised by the period's last day: ordered, received, invoiced, still to invoice — by order, vendor or delivery month); `po_received_not_invoiced` (accepted and not billed by an approved invoice by that day, at the order's prices and VAT — by order, vendor or budget category); `po_match_exceptions` (invoices recorded in the period against an order that do not match, or were let through — by invoice, vendor or kind of issue). Companies, orders and amounts only.

### Registries, privacy and audit

- **Errors:** `PURCHASE_ORDER_NOT_FOUND | _STATE | _SELF_APPROVAL | _NEEDS_APPROVAL | _APPROVAL_INVALID | _SOURCE_INVALID | _REVISION_PENDING | _LINE_BELOW_USED`, `GOODS_RECEIPT_EXCEEDS_ORDER | _NOT_FOUND`, `VENDOR_INVOICE_PO_REQUIRED`, `INVOICE_MATCH_BLOCKED | _NOT_BLOCKED`, `MATCH_SELF_OVERRIDE`. **Reasons:** `purchaseOrderReject`, `purchaseOrderCancel`, `purchaseOrderAmend`, `goodsRejection`, `matchOverride`, and `corrected` in `vendorInvoiceCancel` (mirrored in `packages/api/src/reasons.ts`, each in its CHECK).
- **Notifications** (never an amount): `purchase_order.submitted` (needs a decision), `.decided`, `.sent` (the vendor's contractors). **Counters:** PO- and GR- from the procurement counters. **File purpose** `goods_receipt_photo`.
- **Audit** (ids, numbers, codes, quantities, days and amounts; a note, a delivery note and a decision's words as `{ changed }`): `purchase_order.raised|approved|rejected|cancelled|amended|revision_approved|revision_rejected|revision_withdrawn|remainder_cancelled|closed|sent|acknowledged`, `goods_receipt.recorded`, `vendor_invoice.match_overridden`, `purchase_order_settings.updated`.
- **Privacy:** a vendor is a company (ADR 0077); an order, a receipt and a match are the compound's business records, naming staff as ADR 0046 names a recorder. Photos are of goods. Every response is no-store (a receipt carries signed photo links). No new per-request query.

### Lock order

The caller → the purchase request (update) → the order (update) → the vendor (shared) → the threshold's row (shared) → the approval request → its claim lock → the lines' budgets (shared) and lines (update, in id order) → the stock items (update, in id order) → the procurement counter → the stores' levels. An invoice against an order locks the order before the invoice (as a contract is locked first); a receipt and an invoice's approval never decide on the same quantities at once.

## Assumptions (recorded for the owner; the owner gave no numbers)

1. **Tolerances:** price 2 %, quantity 0 %, amount 100 EGP — a unit price may exceed the order's by up to 2 % **and** a line may vary by up to 100 EGP; a quantity may not exceed what was accepted or ordered at all. All the compound's to change.
2. **An order is never required by default**; when set, it applies to invoices with no contract only.
3. **Approval is separate from the request's:** an order raised from an approved request is approved again, by someone other than its raiser, on the order's own gross (the request's board approval is reused when it covers the order).
4. **Even under the board's approval, the raiser does not approve.**
5. **A lower price never blocks**; a higher one blocks beyond either tolerance; an extra line is held to the amount tolerance.
6. **Receipts accrue nothing in the ledger** (above); what arrived and is not invoiced stays in the commitment.
7. **A credit note from the vendor is handled as a corrected invoice**: the invoice is called off with the reason `corrected` and the right one recorded. A vendor credit-note register (a debit to payables) is not built.
8. **A stock line is received at the order's unit price** (net of VAT), so the item's average cost moves exactly as for ADR 0065's manual receipt.
9. **The vendor's acknowledgement is a record**, not a condition: nothing waits for it.
10. **Sending is a record and a notice to the vendor's contractors, with the PDF to download** — an email with the PDF attached is not built (the mailer has no attachments).
11. **An award's order** draws on the winning bid's amount in all, and only when the award's contract keeps no unpaid payment schedule.

## Deviations for the owner

1. **Gross, not net, is committed and approved** — the invoice's total includes VAT, and a commitment is released by it.
2. **Revisions do not re-approve a price decrease differently:** every revision is approved again, up or down.
3. **Mistaken receipts are not reversed** (a return to the vendor, a counting error): a correction is the stock's adjustment (ADR 0065) and, for the match, an amendment or an override. Open.
4. **A role that raises orders needs the pickers' permissions** (vendors, budget lines, stock) to use the manager's form fully; from a request or an award the source supplies the vendor and line. The budget lines were the maintenance supervisor's gap; see the update below.

## Consequences

- Four migrations (`20270702090000`…`090300`): the file purpose; seven tables under RLS, forced, with composite keys (`purchase_orders`, `purchase_order_lines`, `purchase_order_revisions`, `goods_receipts`, `goods_receipt_lines`, `goods_receipt_photos`, `finance_invoice_match_overrides`); the commitments', the releases', the invoices' (source, state, move-forward trigger) and the procurement counters' CHECKs restated with every value before them; `finance_settings` carries four columns; `finance_vendor_invoices.purchase_order_id`, the invoice lines' `purchase_order_line_id`, `quantity`, `unit_price`; `stock_movements.goods_receipt_id`; the staff inbox's CHECKs restated last.
- The manager's pages: "Purchase orders" (list, raise, the order, receiving, settings), the match panel and the order source on the vendor invoice pages.
- The vendor-invoice view gains `purchaseOrderId` and the lines' `purchaseOrderLineId`, `quantity`, `unitPrice`; commitments and the payment schedule gain the `purchase_order` source.

## Open

- Returns to the vendor and reversal of a mistaken receipt; a vendor credit-note register; a service confirmed through the technician's ticket.
- An order's email to the vendor with the PDF attached; the vendor's price list or catalog; blanket orders; a purchase request's lines.
- Accruing receipts in the ledger once stock is valued (a goods-received-not-invoiced account and a price variance account).
- Match tolerances per vendor or per budget category; requiring an order by category.

## Update 2026-11-11: who raises an order reads the budgets

The owner widened the maintenance supervisor's reading, so the whole order form is theirs.

- `GET /staff/finance/budgets` and `GET /staff/finance/budgets/{id}` take `finance.budget` **or** `purchase_orders.raise`. Who raises an order reads the budgets and their lines, with what each has left, to name the line it is paid from.
- Everything else on the budget stays with `finance.budget`: drafting and submitting a budget, its lines and months, the settings, the expenses and their authorizations.
- The maintenance supervisor already read the vendors (`assets.manage`, `purchases.request`) and the stock (`stock.manage`), so a direct order is now theirs from start to end. No role gained a permission.
- The accountant still reads no stock items or stores (`stock.manage`), so the accountant's orders have no stock lines.
